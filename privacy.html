Privacy Policy

Track Record is a music trivia game designed to be played without creating an account or providing personal information.

Information We Collect

Track Record does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party data services.

Game Data

Gameplay takes place on your device. Track Record does not send your answers, scores, or activity to the developer or to any third party.

Children's Privacy

Because Track Record does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If Track Record's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
